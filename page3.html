The Rabbit and the Tortoise - Page 3

Meanwhile, the tortoise trudged on steadily, his eyes fixed on the path ahead. He did not let the rabbit’s earlier mockery discourage him. Every step he took brought him closer to the finish line. The forest grew quieter as the day went on, and soon, the tortoise spotted the riverbank in the distance. The rabbit, still snoozing under the tree, remained oblivious to the tortoise’s progress. The spectators, who had scattered to explore the forest, began returning to the path as they saw the tortoise nearing the end.
