The Rabbit and the Tortoise - Page 1

Once upon a time, in a lush green forest, a rabbit and a tortoise lived as neighbors. The rabbit, swift and agile, often boasted about his speed to everyone he met. The tortoise, known for his calm demeanor and patience, remained unaffected by the rabbit’s taunts. One day, the rabbit, feeling particularly overconfident, mocked the tortoise’s slow pace once again. “Why don’t we have a race?” suggested the tortoise, smiling. The rabbit burst into laughter. “A race? Against you? This will be over before you take ten steps!” Despite his mockery, he accepted the challenge. The other animals in the forest gathered to witness the spectacle.
