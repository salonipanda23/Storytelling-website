The Rabbit and the Tortoise - Page 2

The day of the race arrived. The starting point was marked near a large oak tree, and the finish line was set near the riverbank. At the signal, the rabbit bolted forward, leaving the tortoise far behind. The spectators cheered, but most of them assumed the outcome was already clear. The rabbit, confident he would win, decided to stop midway to nibble on some fresh clover. Seeing no sign of the tortoise, he stretched out under a shady tree for a nap. “This race is as good as mine,” he thought before drifting off to sleep.
